A landmark month: the Potato Virus Testing Service launched in full, two flagship summer events landed, and campaigns, SEO and social all gathered pace.

Created and implemented the marketing strategy for the Potato Virus Testing Service, including the landing page, media release, marketing email, social content, event collateral and a full page advert for the PiP programme.
